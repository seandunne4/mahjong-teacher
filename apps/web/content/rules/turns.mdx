<Rule id="turns.order" title="Turn order is counter-clockwise" setting="seating.turnDirection">
  <Diagram hand="E S W N" caption="East → South → West → North" />
  <Summary>The dealer (East) goes first; play passes to the player on your right: East, South, West, North.</Summary>
  <Full>

Seats follow the Chinese order East-South-West-North, which runs counter-clockwise around the table. That's not how a map reads: South sits on East's *right*.

  </Full>
  <Edge>

Claims can jump the order (see *Claiming*); play always continues from the claimer's right.

  </Edge>
</Rule>

<Rule id="turns.dealer-start" title="The dealer starts by discarding" setting="deal">
  <Diagram hand="1112345678999m 5m" caption="The dealer starts with 14 tiles and discards one" />
  <Summary>The dealer starts with 14 tiles and opens by discarding one. Everyone else has 13.</Summary>
  <Full>

Before the first discard, anyone holding flower or season tiles exposes and replaces them. Then the dealer discards face up in the centre, leaving them with 13 like everyone else.

  </Full>
  <Edge>

If the dealer's 14 tiles already make a winning hand, they can declare Mahjong immediately.

  </Edge>
</Rule>

<Rule id="turns.each" title="On your turn" setting="claims">
  <Diagram hand="23m 66p | 4m*" caption="Either claim the last discard, or draw from the live wall" />
  <Summary>Either claim the last discard (if allowed) or draw from the live wall, then discard one tile.</Summary>
  <Full>

1. If the previous discard completes a set you can claim, or wins you the hand, you may claim it.
2. Otherwise draw the next tile from the live wall.
3. Discard one tile face up into the centre.

Every discard is visible to everyone, so other players will soon guess what you're collecting.

  </Full>
  <Edge>

You always end your turn with 13 tiles in hand plus any exposed sets (a kong counts as 3 for this purpose).

  </Edge>
</Rule>

<Rule id="turns.flowers" title="Drawing a flower or season" setting="bonusTiles" when={{ "derived.hasBonus": true }}>
  <Diagram hand="1f | 2x" caption="Expose bonus tiles and draw replacements" />
  <Summary>Expose it immediately, set it aside, and draw a replacement from the dead wall before you discard.</Summary>
  <Full>

Bonus tiles never stay in your hand. Each one is worth <Val path="bonusTiles.fanEach" /> fan at scoring, added after the hand meets the minimum.

  </Full>
  <Edge>

If the replacement is also a bonus tile, expose it and draw again. If the dead wall runs out, draw replacements from <Val path="deadWall.onExhausted" />.

<When path="bonusTiles.allEightWins" is={true}>House rule: collecting all eight flowers and seasons wins the hand on the spot.</When>

  </Edge>
</Rule>
